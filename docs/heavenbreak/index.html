THE WORLD / SHELTER: HEAVEN

A place to return to.

49 days. One promise.

“Hold on for seven weeks. I'll return on the morning of day fifty.”

After the lord leaves for Terrarium, Shelter: HEAVEN stands isolated on the northern frontier. Supplies and support have stopped. Chii and Lisa face relentless Riptor attacks, holding on to the promise of his return.

Keep the shelter standing. Keep a place for him to come home to.

SHELTER OPERATOR

Chii

A government-dispatched operator who keeps the shelter running. Meticulous and composed, she hides a caring heart behind her cool manner.

VETERAN NAU

Lisa

The shelter's most experienced Nau. Brave and quick to judge a battlefield, she holds the line while waiting for the lord's return.
